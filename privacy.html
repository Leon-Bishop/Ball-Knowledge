Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Ball Knowledge (ballknowledge.life) handles information when you use our football quiz. Contact us at dev.leonb@gmail.com about privacy questions or requests.

Information and game preferences

You can play without registering a traditional account or providing an email address. The game uses your browser's local storage to remember settings such as language, theme, selected leagues and best score. Clearing browser storage may reset this information.

Optional player names and leaderboards

If you choose an optional player name, our leaderboard service can process your chosen username, scores, a randomly generated browser identity token, and approximate country or region based on network information. The server stores a hash of the identity token rather than its original value. Usernames, scores and approximate location labels may appear publicly in rankings. The service does not request your precise GPS location. Network-derived information may also be used for abuse prevention and rate limiting.

Because identity is tied to browser storage, clearing it can prevent access to the same leaderboard identity. For questions about leaderboard information or deletion requests, email dev.leonb@gmail.com.

Advertising and cookies

Ball Knowledge integrates Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads. Third parties may collect or receive information from your browser in connection with ads. Learn more at Google's advertising technologies page and manage Google's personalized advertising at My Ad Center.

We use browser storage for game features; third-party providers may separately use cookies or other storage. You can manage or block cookies using your browser settings, though some functions may be affected. Where legally required, advertising-related storage or personalized advertising should be subject to appropriate consent choices.

Service providers and technical information

Our website is hosted with GitHub Pages. Optional shared leaderboards use Cloudflare Workers and database services. Some league images are served by external providers. These services may receive technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata when your browser connects to them. Their processing is governed by their applicable policies.

Purposes and retention

Information is used to operate the quiz, remember settings, provide optional rankings, maintain security, prevent abuse and support advertising. Browser settings remain until cleared or changed. Leaderboard data and provider logs may be retained according to operational requirements and applicable provider practices; retention durations are not represented here as fixed. Contact us to enquire about your data or request deletion.

Your choices and rights

You can play without choosing a username, clear game information from your browser, manage advertising preferences, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information where applicable. Rights and lawful bases for processing depend on your location and the processing involved. Email dev.leonb@gmail.com for assistance.

Children

Ball Knowledge is a general-audience football game, not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not intentionally request children's real names or email addresses through the game. Please contact us if you have concerns about a child's information.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet service is completely secure. Providers may process information in other countries, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website, providers or requirements change. The current version and its revision date will be published on this page.
